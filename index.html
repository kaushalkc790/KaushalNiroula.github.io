VehicleTelemetry — Fleet Failure Risk Analytics & Power BI Dashboard

Built an end-to-end fleet analytics stack from raw CAN-bus sensors through a Power BI operations dashboard and a live scoring app, so maintenance risk can be reviewed by vehicle and by subsystem rather than in a raw log dump.

  • Quality-checked 1,970 readings from 50 vehicles across 10 brands, then scored engine, brake, and battery alerts that are rare and mutually exclusive.
  • Kept a transparent one-rule baseline as champion (oil, brake fluid, and voltage thresholds) after logistic regression and random forest failed to beat grouped out-of-fold scores.
  • Cross-checked the same cleaned rows in PostgreSQL with 35 labelled queries, exported to Excel.
  • Designed a four-page Power BI report (28 DAX measures): fleet overview, subsystem risk, vehicle deep dive, and operational insights.
  • Shipped a Flask app that scores a single vehicle or a CSV and returns High / Medium / Low risk bands using the same champion rules.
